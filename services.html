The "Publication-Ready" MD Suites

At Levitt Dynamics, our signature Molecular Dynamics suites are engineered for researchers aiming for high-impact journals. Every suite is an end-to-end standard MD service designed to take you from your starting coordinates to a polished manuscript draft.

The Core Engine: Included in Every Suite

  • Dual-State Simulation Pipeline: We run continuous standard MD on both your Apo-Protein (unbound state) and your Protein-Ligand Complex to provide a perfect comparative baseline.
  • Publication-Grade Analytics: High-resolution plotting of RMSD, RMSF, Radius of Gyration (Rg), SASA, and rigorous Hydrogen-bond profiling.
  • Scientific Write-up: Professional drafting of the computational Methodology and Figure Captions, formatted and ready to be inserted directly into your manuscript.

🟢 The Kinetic Suite

Optimized for fundamental structural biology.

Engineered for standard, compact globular proteins and single-domain enzymes. The Kinetic Suite provides rapid, highly accurate trajectory generation for straightforward targets.

👉 Ideal for: Standard enzymes, kinases, and fundamental stability studies.

🔵 The Momentum Suite

Built for heavy biological machinery.

When your research moves beyond standard enzymes, the Momentum Suite takes over. Designed to handle large multi-domain architectures and intricate protein-protein complexes that require expanded simulation boxes.

👉 Ideal for: Dimeric/tetrameric complexes, elongated proteins, and larger multi-ligand environments.

🟣 The Resonance Suite

Designed for complex structural environments.

Our highest-tier standard suite, built specifically for massive atomic counts and complex solvation requirements.

👉 Ideal for: Transmembrane proteins (GPCRs, Ion Channels) and viral capsids.

⚫ The Continuum Custom

Limitless scale for bespoke computational projects.

For massive macromolecular assemblies or entirely custom multi-mutant experimental designs that defy standard categorization.

👉 Ideal for: Custom timescales, non-standard environments, and bespoke analyses.
