Our Inspiration

"Our work is deeply inspired by the pioneering efforts of Dr. Michael Levitt, who co-received the 2013 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for the development of multiscale models for complex chemical systems."

In line with his lifelong dedication to making molecular structure modeling a highly practical, accessible tool for the physical sciences, Levitt Dynamics was founded to help academic researchers visualize and analyze biomolecular interactions over time—providing high-fidelity data without the paralyzing initial overhead of expensive laboratory trials.
